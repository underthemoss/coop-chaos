# Roadmap

## Current priority: Chicken Civ design, then creative work

On 24 October 2026 the user selected warfare and territorial conquest as the main
goal and requested detailed design documents and narrative before creative design.
The [gameplay specification](../story/proposals/chicken-civ-design.md) covers
resources, production, exploration, combat, capture, supply and victory. The
[narrative proposal](../story/proposals/chicken-civ-narrative.md) covers motives,
societies and persistent consequences. Specific mechanics, cast and numbers remain
provisional. The [research study](../story/proposals/chicken-civ-starcraft-gameplay.md)
explains the StarCraft comparison; the [creative handoff](../art-direction/chicken-civ-creative-handoff.md)
defines the next design phase. This sequence supersedes the earlier art-first
priority. Existing implementation and open art acceptance are described below;
this design packet does not implement warfare or authorize a release.

The [Long Crow progression specification](../story/proposals/chicken-civ-progression.md)
now details the proposed tech dependencies, personal learning, command hierarchy,
succession and campaign persistence. Prototype the base conquest loop first,
then three optional techniques and one authored Veteran specialty; earned careers
and succession need an explicitly persistent multi-scenario proof.

## Current bounded economy status

The implemented scope now includes renewable reeds, typed staple/protein food,
physical local nursery deliveries, a covered grounded nursery after a home,
named willing-hen support, actual fertility and laying, supplied incubation,
chicks, grounded juveniles and adult workers. Care removes an adult from ordinary
work and establishes a persistent named bond and home attachment. An automatic
four-minute cycle, pause, 1×/4× and conservative food/housing forecasts make
constraints visible. Six reachable adult places per tree remain the maximum
implemented layout; larger household targets are future studies. Structural wood,
cultivation, threats, politics and powered access remain future.

[Settlement design](../story/settlement-design.md) owns the accepted rules and provisional numbers. This
extends the inhabited-tree proof; it does not prove thirty-minute enjoyment or
complete balance. Young stay grounded until adulthood; upper broodrooms await
real access.

## Construction proof now

Named-tree Build cards now expose local work and residents. Completed homes can
gain a two-berth sleeping deck or a hauling-basket prototype. The first real lift
teaches the shared technique; completed baskets raise later deck materials.
This is a bounded demonstrated invention, not a general research or power system.

Scale-first habitat work keeps adults independent of tree size and structures
in true metres, while preserving original adult mobility and six reachable berths per tree and existing saves.
The separate [household planning targets](../story/settlement-design.md#scale-sleeping-places-and-future-households)
(Lowfork24, Splitcrown16, Reedside12 sheltered residents) require usable layout,
care and food support before population rules expand. Static occupancy studies
are design evidence, not a claim that those residents already exist in play.


A working renewable-reed chain supports the first earned woven treehouse: place a
trunk site, reserve, collect, freely walk, visibly carry, deliver and
consume. Supporting store nests hold local goods used in later builds. Natural
branches work before construction; explicit saved evening/day demonstrates
fly-up/down, earned ladder access, finite sheltered capacity, rest quality and
contributors' persistent attachment. Four adults and numerical tuning are provisional.
Interruption, cancellation and mid-work saves keep goods physically accounted for.
The old authored choice remains a historical fixture with a separate retained save.

Construction centers on forest treehouse homes and access; open commons remain
productive no-man's-land rather than a housing grid. Current landscape geography
does not implement kingdoms, complete nutrition, danger or territorial ownership.
[Life above the ground](../story/technology.md) develops future canopy neighborhoods,
powered lifts, science-fiction elevators and distinct defenses.

## Existing art foundation: acceptance remains open

The [opening-view brief](../art-direction/opening-view-exploration.md) owns the
concrete artifact inventory and opening-view-v2 visual bar for the living-grove
foundation. Its bounded actions include basic foraging and building: rest/open a food
place, commission a treehouse at an eligible tree, inspect individuals, and watch
autonomous activity. These are independent actions, not an authored opening arc,
mandatory build order or scripted First Home sequence. Existing free movement, stores,
construction, roosts and saves are reusable supporting capabilities.

**Visual acceptance remains open.** The rejected Node-authored pass has been
replaced with editable Blender assets and action clips. Follow the
[asset pipeline](../art-direction/asset-pipeline.md) to produce an editable hero
tree, representative bird with movement/work poses, resource bundle and woven
home/access sample. Judge close artifact views and their combined actual-camera
scene against v2 before expanding to the full cast, forest and resource/building
kit. Tool installation, successful loading and a similar layout do not establish
visual fidelity. Keep fixed responsive framing, with no pan or zoom controls.

Polished movement and work poses must make autonomous life credible, including
natural flight before construction and earned ladder use. Integrate the wider
artifacts only after the route proves anatomy, canopy volume, materials, lighting,
attachment alignment and measured runtime performance. Preserve saved identities,
progress, cargo and occupancy through art replacement. Concepts are not GLBs,
rigs or evidence of a playable scene.

Finite construction-reed gathering now uses spatial patches, reservations,
atomic extraction, visible cargo and local storage, with strict versioned saves.
Fresh worlds start with eight loose reeds and two twelve-reed patches; these
counts are provisional. Three open mixed-food places now support autonomous
feeding and browsing, appetite, provisional individual habitat preferences and
remembered productive places. Food recovers on bounded daytime simulation ticks, even while browsed;
food and reeds have separate ledgers. Player-created roads and connectivity
are removed. Strict settlement-5 saves migrate versions one through four without dropping prior work and movement progress.
Complete nutrition, richer food ecology, pecking-order effects and predators remain future. The bounded nursery loop above implements reproduction. Browser proof includes feeding choice, Rest/Open,
free carry/build, night access, refresh, narrow layout and reduced motion.

## Later tree and chicken systems

[Tree kinships](../story/tree-kinships.md) establishes named tree characters,
botanical families and open affiliation. Prototype one observable chicken-to-tree
tie and a distinct community-to-tree response before building a family progression
system. Exact family names, consciousness expression and unlock recipes remain
proposals. Mixed-family opportunities require complementary places, materials,
knowledge and actual work; prove a viable single-family or trade alternative.
Future low-flight birds need usable natural low perches and access, without
silently changing the four current adults or their saved behavior.

## Later playable proof: economy sustaining territorial conquest

Follow the accepted [settlement design](../story/settlement-design.md). Build a
small contest between two coops and three strategic groves, with meaningful
resource placement, roosts, gathering, local stores, equipment production and
visible autonomous deliveries. The [detailed proposal](../story/proposals/chicken-civ-design.md)
starts with twelve named adults per side, three combat roles, scouting, physical
field supply and a central capture objective. These are proposed test parameters,
not a change to the current four-adult fixture. Mobilization must compete with
economic work; shortages, casualties and occupation must have visible causes.
Predator defense alone cannot qualify the conquest goal. Brood growth is a longer
investment, not instant military replacement. Exact rates and balance need testing.

The current thin construction-delivery journey already supplies a reusable part
of this milestone: place a site, reserve and collect local material,
carry and deliver it,
consume it through construction, and expose useful output. Prove resource
conservation, blocked-job explanations, interruption, and coherent saved
in-flight work before adding several chains. This is a verification journey,
not a mandatory player sequence or a mandate for a new engine framework.

Connect production, scouting, mobilization, fighting and consolidation, then
playtest a roughly thirty-minute contest. Compare early aggression, economic
investment and defensive counterattack across more than one map. Fun is
unproven. The individuality test is recognizing a bird by its actions, changing
a layout/staffing decision partly because of it, and feeling its loss through
both economic and emotional consequences. A second dialogue scene is not a gate.
Production character rigs should improve readability of work and encounters in
the actual camera as the loop develops.

## Later proofs

Several districts develop local relationships and logistical dependence; extend
the rival-coop foundation with inhabited occupation and concrete access or defense pacts. Kinship, marriage, fostering,
and succession grow from those functioning communities and retain named-character
agency. Longer generation calendars and save migrations need explicit design.
Larger campaigns, advanced warfare and any multiplayer follow the bounded
single-player conquest proof; no
backend, deployment, or delivery dates are implied by this roadmap.
