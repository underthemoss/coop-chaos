# Visual bible

## Direction

**Tree art reset, 2026-10-16:** the user explicitly rejected the current tree
look. The older procedural trees were functional placeholders, not an approved
visual target or a constraint on replacement art. Follow the fresh
[tree design exploration](tree-design-exploration.md) for the user's accepted
visual direction: sculptural spreading trees whose exposed branch anatomy can become
inhabited neighborhoods. Its growth forms and generated boards guide an asset
proof, not completed game assets. Botanical species-based families and named tree
characterhood belong to [tree kinships](../story/tree-kinships.md); growth form,
family and individual ancestry must remain visually and conceptually distinct.
Prove one Longbough at the actual camera before
replacing the forest. Keep natural roosting, earned treehouse construction, and
readable ground-to-canopy access central.

[Opening-view exploration](opening-view-exploration.md) combines this tree language
and the proposed chickens into a fresh-start composition. Version 2 is the preferred
concept: a whole crown, open work court, finite cache and restrained edge controls.
Its concepts remain aspirational references and v2 is the visual acceptance bar
for the art-led living sandbox. The current Node-authored GLB pass was rejected
against that bar; working rules and loading do not make it accepted art. The
brief owns the required artifact inventory and authoring-route proof. Retain
Lowfork and saved identities rather than renaming a progressed tree Morrow.
Basic foraging, building and inspection support autonomous activity without an
authored opening plot or mandatory sequence; forage details remain proposals.

A small inhabited world with comic dignity: moss, ochre, warm cream, bark, and
soft evening light. The user’s visual reference is
[Kingdoms and Castles, official store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/569480/Kingdoms_and_Castles/):
an elevated strategy view of a broad inhabited landscape, faceted terrain,
forest masses, shoreline/water, and simple readable geometry. Derive those spatial
qualities in an original chicken world; do not copy its artwork, medieval buildings,
UI, or human society. This foundation does not claim its production quality.

Readable silhouettes and expressive posture matter more
than surface detail. The default camera looks down at a fixed angle, orthographic or gently
perspectival, onto a true 3D world. The world dominates play space; use a restrained
inspector for character and story context. The current slice uses a fixed elevated
view with responsive framing and no pan, zoom or rotation controls. Art and
lighting must make birds, routes and canopy use legible in that framing. Avoid
a close-up circular character stage or a landing-page layout in the play view. Characters must hold up as modeled, animated
forms from every visible direction. Flat standees are not the target.

Chickens build with beaks and feet: woven reeds, twigs, nesting grass, thornworks,
covered routes, and branch crossings integrate with trunks, boughs, roots and terrain. These
purposeful structures support the [settlement design](../story/settlement-design.md).
Show construction progress, cargo, jobs, transport junctions, and route cover at
the strategy camera's scale. Natural refuges still matter. No human medieval
assets, farmers, or borrowed human barns. Coops are social identities; chicken-invented machinery can evolve with the technology design.

Work must be readable before detail: where a carrier is heading, what it carries,
which site is unfinished, and where a route is exposed. Repeated posture, pace,
companionship, and protective behavior should reveal individuals without forcing
biography reading. The current proof conveys work with seven authored rigid-articulation clips and visible cargo, not production skeletal animation.

Character design must express individuality without mapping worth to beauty,
sex, color, or size. Hens need differentiated carriage and presence. A rooster's
protectiveness can read as an offered wing or vigilant stance; rival swagger can
still conceal fear. Earn comedy through timing and attitude, not disposable lives.

## Character and animation brief

[Chicken design exploration](chicken-design-exploration.md) develops fictional
breed-inspired lineages and individual expression. They remain chickens, not
separate species, job castes or moral categories. Preserve an individual's
silhouette, wear, habits and history without making lineage dictate allegiance.
Tree affinities can change, and different bodies must have legible usable access.
The four opening adults can fly; this fixture does not establish flight for all
future lineages. Concepts alone do not prove animation or locomotion.

Production characters: Blender meshes, rigs, materials, and authored clips,
exported as GLB. Begin with recognizable breast, head, beak, comb, wing, feet,
and tail silhouettes. Give individuals visual identifiers and an emotional range.
Design for the angled gameplay camera before investing in close-up detail.

First useful clips: idle/breathe, walk, carry/drag, gather, weave/build,
look/attention, peck, alarm, protective
wing, yielding, indignant recovery, and settling to rest. These are intentions,
not completed assets. Transitions should reflect who is watching whom. Ensure
wing and head poses communicate at gameplay scale; do not rely only on UI text.
Honor reduced-motion settings and keep story feedback understandable without
animation or color alone.

## Current proof and acceptance

The following records functional behavior. Both the older procedural trees and
the replacement Node-authored GLB pass were visually rejected. They remain
functional placeholders. Acceptance requires actual high-quality artifacts, close
views and the live fixed-camera scene compared with opening-view-v2; functional
checks or a similar layout alone do not establish art quality.

Four original GLB adult mesh proofs inhabit a broad faceted landscape. Three branching
landmarks sit in small forest home clusters around the glade: Lowfork, Splitcrown
and Reedside. A reed cache lies across open common ground. These are map geography,
not simulated kingdoms or a safety boundary. Current mixed-food places support bounded autonomous foraging; broader field ecology remains future.
Treehouses rest on supported forks and boughs; the tree origin is only their placement reference. Supporting stores and transport occupy the ground.

Show a woven branch deck, crooked readable ladder, rain hood, local reed stacks,
visible carried bundles and free ground travel. Delivered material, deck, roof and
ladder stages precede useful sheltered occupancy. Natural branches work before
building: adults fly up/down; improved access uses a visible approach and ladder
climb, then the supported home edge and ramp for the upper deck. Evening light makes the saved phase legible. Shelter does not imply
unimplemented universal defense. Readiness, work and remembered contribution have
written feedback as well as motion. The current birds use seven authored rigid-articulation clips, not production rigs
or completed character art.

[Technology design](../story/technology.md) extends the chicken aesthetic toward
canopy links, counterweight hauling, powered lifts, and late science-fiction
elevators. Preserve ingenuity, branches, grasping perches, useful landing space
and distinctive material shapes as inventions grow. Human housing assets remain
inappropriate; machinery itself is welcome.

Before acceptance, inspect silhouettes and cargo at the fixed gameplay framing,
readable travel and construction, natural and improved occupancy, flight/ladder
use, names and selection, fixed responsive framing, narrow layout, and non-motion work
feedback. Reduced motion suppresses decorative bob and flutter; essential travel
and textual state remain. Avoid a generic dashboard or a close-up character stage.


The inhabited-tree proof uses the content-owned [habitat contract](../src/content/tree-habitats.json)
for natural roost, home fork, hauling cleft and upper sleeping deck. Anatomy must
support those places and preserve visible crown windows, occupied faces and the
cargo shaft. A ground workcourt is useful space beside the trunk, not transit
through its center. Lowfork anatomy and open rainhoods are incremental runtime
art improvements; neither concept parity nor new tree food/shade/defense mechanics
follows from them.
