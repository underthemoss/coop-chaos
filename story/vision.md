# A small world. Long memories.

## Established premise

Coop Chaos personifies human social and political drama through chickens.
They have daily lives, pride, affection, fear, ambition, grudges, and loyalty.
Comedy comes from taking their dignity seriously. Emotional credibility keeps
choices from becoming an optimization spreadsheet.

There are no humans or farmers. Coops are tribes and communities, not necessarily
human-built sheds. Hens have lives and a pecking order. Roosters can be chivalrous
and protective inside their coop, and antagonistic toward rivals unless pacts
change that relationship. Eggs hatch into the next generation; they are not sold.

Kinship and marriage moves between coops can secure alliances. Named hens retain
preferences, relationships, loyalties, and consequences through those moves.
Arrangements may be welcomed, resisted, coerced, or betrayed. Their agency must
be represented even when they cannot control every event. No character is a
fungible resource.

Human patterns provide fictional inspiration across many cultures and
individual experiences. They are not claims that all people, hens, or roosters
behave in one way. Literal zoology does not outrank the social story.

## Experience to protect

A player should understand what happened, whose need is in conflict, what they
can choose, and who will remember it. Small daily disputes can expose larger
politics. Shelter can mean safety, a customary seat can mean recognition, and
an alliance can preserve peace while changing someone's home.

Stats support visible drama. Do not add a loyalty meter without a relationship
and behavior that explains it. Prefer one legible consequence to a page of
anonymous modifiers. Avoid deterministic moral labels that declare one choice
universally good.

## Present scope

Browser, single player first, multiplayer later. Fully modeled animated chickens
in a true 3D world viewed from an elevated strategy camera. The user’s
[Kingdoms and Castles visual reference](https://store.steampowered.com/app/569480/Kingdoms_and_Castles/)
means a broad, low-poly inhabited landscape, not a close-up character pedestal.
World scale must not erase the named individuals, their motives, or visible
consequences. Its human medieval buildings and economics are not our premise. The current procedural meshes
are stand-ins that prove rendering and integration, not the intended final art.
The initial evening, cast, community name, and UI prose are provisional design
examples. They do not establish additional canon merely by appearing in code.
